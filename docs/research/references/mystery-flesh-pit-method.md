# Reference: Mystery Flesh Pit National Park

Source role: **worldbuilding-method reference only**

## Separation rule

This project uses an **original setting**.

Mystery Flesh Pit National Park is not canon and must not supply direct:
- names
- companies
- park identity
- creatures
- attractions
- maps
- historical events
- terminology
- organizations
- continuity

The useful material is the **method used to make a giant organism feel like a mundane human environment**.

## 1. The fiction is presented as artifacts, not exposition

The official site describes itself as a collection of artifacts and ephemera relating to the defunct site.

That framing matters.

The world is communicated through things that appear to have had practical purposes:
- maps
- notices
- warning labels
- signage
- corporate/park material
- equipment
- souvenirs
- ecological documentation

### flesh-pit application

Our original setting should prefer **objects with jobs** over lore dumps.

Examples:
- restroom maintenance sheet
- depth warning sign
- obsolete evacuation map
- tissue-contact procedure
- broken equipment label
- mutation symptom poster
- old staff graffiti
- pipe identification plate

Each should exist because somebody in-world needed it.

---

## 2. Mundane systems normalize impossible biology

One of MFP's strongest tricks is collision between:
- colossal impossible organism
- ordinary bureaucracy and infrastructure

The official material supports:
- park branding
- hazard signage
- surface facilities
- industrial branding
- tourism/souvenir language

This makes the organism feel bigger than a monster encounter.
Humans have built routines around it.

### flesh-pit application

Our restroom/base should imply that the bizarre activity is not occurring in a blank horror void.

Possible original-world signals:
- standardized fixtures installed against tissue
- maintenance access
- depth measurements
- safety color coding
- old employee instructions
- institutional typography
- repaired/replaced wall sections
- pipes disappearing into biological matter

Do not explain the institution in detail before the core loop needs it.

---

## 3. Biology is treated as geography

The useful abstraction is:

> anatomy can be named, mapped, traversed, engineered around, and treated like terrain

This helps our depth progression.

### flesh-pit application

Depth regions should feel like **places**, not texture swaps.

A region can be defined by:
- dominant tissue
- motion
- sound
- fluid behavior
- geometry
- human infrastructure residue
- biological hazard
- optimal excavation verb

Example abstract categories:
- compressible soft layer
- directional contractile layer
- vascular zone
- dense structural zone
- cavity system

Avoid directly copying anatomical/park names from MFP.

---

## 4. Biology is also infrastructure

The official ecology material does more than describe strange fauna.
For example, the Aquifer Leech entry describes:
- habitat
- feeding behavior
- ecological function
- human technological/medical use

This is an important worldbuilding pattern:

**weird organism → ecological role → human exploitation**

### flesh-pit application

Original biological phenomena should ideally have at least two of:
- ecological function
- excavation consequence
- infrastructure consequence
- mutation consequence

Example:
- a vessel-cleaning organism is not just decoration
- it may reduce local bleeding
- its absence may create clots
- eating it may affect a mutation path

This is stronger than adding collectible monster lore.

---

## 5. Warning design implies mechanics

Official MFP merchandise includes hazard-sign concepts around:
- digestion
- bone areas
- burns
- off-trail danger
- acidic environments
- night advisories

The specific signs are not ours to copy.

The method is useful:
**a warning sign can teach both world and mechanic simultaneously.**

### flesh-pit application

Original warning labels can foreshadow:
- tissue contraction
- vessel rupture
- pressure changes
- corrosive fluids
- unstable cavities

A sign is valuable when the player later experiences the thing it warned about.

Do not create fake signage only as decoration.

---

## 6. Scale is communicated indirectly

The organism feels huge not merely because a wall is large.

Scale is implied by:
- maps
- facilities
- multiple human uses
- specialized ecological zones
- infrastructure built over time
- organizations treating regions as routine destinations

### flesh-pit application

Because our game is small, we should not build a physically gigantic open world.

Instead imply scale cheaply:
- depth counter
- old maps that extend far beyond playable area
- pipes/elevators continuing elsewhere
- massive biological motion heard but not seen
- infrastructure labeled for unreachable sectors
- restroom built as one tiny service point in a much larger system

This gives large-world feeling without large-world production cost.

---

## 7. The world should not overpower the loop

MFP is primarily a worldbuilding project.
Our game is primarily a compact gameplay loop.

Therefore:
- do not require reading to understand progress
- do not turn every depth into a lore stop
- do not build dozens of fictional agencies
- do not reproduce an ARG/document archive inside the game

World detail should **ride on top of eating and returning**.

A good artifact is something the player notices while doing the loop.

---

## 8. Original-world design principles extracted from MFP

Safe abstract principles to keep:

1. giant organism functions as terrain
2. humans install mundane infrastructure in biology
3. anatomical regions function like geological regions
4. warnings turn body processes into workplace hazards
5. ecology and industry overlap
6. scale is implied through documentation and unreachable systems
7. institutional normalcy makes grotesque material more uncanny

These principles are not sufficient on their own to define our setting.

---

## 9. What to copy / what not to copy

### Copy only at abstraction level
- artifact-based exposition
- bureaucratic normalcy
- organism-as-geography
- biology-as-workplace-hazard
- ecology tied to human use
- indirect scale communication

### Do not copy
- visual replicas of MFP maps/posters
- recognizable logo/branding structure
- specific creatures
- park/tourism premise as the core setting
- Anodyne or related companies
- named regions/attractions
- disaster chronology
- exact warning graphics or phrases

---

## One-line application to flesh-pit

> **Make the impossible body feel old, serviced, labeled and routine—without making it Mystery Flesh Pit.**
