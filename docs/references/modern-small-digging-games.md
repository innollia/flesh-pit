# Modern Small Digging Success Cases

Purpose: identify what recent small mining/digging games add to the base loop.

Data note: Steam review counts are used only as a rough reception/reach signal. They are **not sales estimates**.

## A Game About Digging A Hole (2025)

Sources:
- https://steamcommunity.com/app/3244220/allnews/
- https://www.pcgamer.com/games/survival-crafting/designed-in-just-a-few-weeks-this-usd5-indie-game-about-digging-a-hole-has-journeyed-to-the-centre-of-steams-top-sellers-chart/

Verified success:
- developer announced **1 million copies sold** by Aug 1, 2025
- reached Steam Top 30 within days of launch
- Twitch peak cited by developer: up to 60,000 simultaneous viewers
- built from a very small/simple concept in a short development period

Core twist:
- almost no twist
- extreme clarity + cheap price + physical 3D hole + upgrade loop

Lesson:
- a digging game does not need huge systemic scope if the fantasy is instantly understandable and clip-friendly

For flesh-pit:
- "I eat a tunnel through a giant body until I'm full, then vomit and mutate" is similarly explainable in one sentence
- protect that trailer-readable simplicity

## Mining Mechs (2023)

Source:
- https://store.steampowered.com/app/1603180/Mining_Mechs/

Current signal:
- around 2,500+ Steam purchaser reviews across all languages
- Very Positive overall

Core twist:
- simple digging loop + up to 8-player online co-op
- multiple mechs
- passive-income mines

Loop:
**dig → collect → surface → sell → upgrade mech → dig deeper**

Lesson:
- extremely conventional progression can still work when one social/identity hook carries it

For flesh-pit:
- do not add co-op merely because it worked here
- our comparable identity hook is the bodily consumption/mutation loop

## DigDigDrill (2024)

Source:
- https://store.steampowered.com/app/2916770/DigDigDrill
- https://steamcommunity.com/app/2916770/discussions/0/4839771250825111159/

Current signal:
- around 600 Steam-purchaser reviews
- about 95% positive

Core twist:
- relaxed mining with no enemy/time pressure
- floor 999 depth goal
- bag-full return loop
- collected ores are fitted into blueprints to **forge custom drills**
- hack-and-slash-style effects and synergies

Community feedback specifically praises the drill crafting system as standing out from ordinary progression.

Lesson:
- upgrade customization can be the deep system while the actual mining remains extremely simple

For flesh-pit:
- mutation tree could fill this role
- however, avoid complex buildcraft unless it changes the way tissues are eaten
- mutations should create excavation synergies, not generic RPG stats

## BORE BLASTERS (2024)

Source:
- https://store.steampowered.com/app/2398170/BORE_BLASTERS/

Current signal:
- roughly 700 Steam purchaser reviews
- around 80% positive

Core twist:
- **machine-gun drilling**
- high-speed destruction rather than slow pickaxe mining
- procedural mines
- permanent progress even after failed runs
- artifacts/biomes/upgrades

Developer explicitly cites Motherload and Galactic Mining Corp as inspirations.

Lesson:
- the tool fantasy itself can carry the game
- changing the *feel* of excavation creates a new subgenre impression even when progression is familiar

For flesh-pit:
- hands → hooked tearing → powered cutter → suction/cautery can be our equivalent
- each tool stage should look different enough to sell in a 3-second clip

## Wall World (2023)

Sources:
- https://alawar.com/games/wall-world
- https://store.steampowered.com/app/2187290/

Current signal:
- roughly 11,000 Steam purchaser reviews across all languages
- Very Positive overall
- expanded into DLC and additional Wall World projects

Core twist:
- Dome Keeper-like mining/defense structure turned sideways
- mobile robospider base travels along a colossal vertical wall
- discrete mines/biomes
- combat and mining share the same upgrade economy

Lesson:
- a strong spatial/world gimmick can make a familiar mining loop marketable again

For flesh-pit:
- giant organism is our spatial gimmick
- but unlike Wall World, the world material itself must affect excavation behavior

## Core Keeper (2022 Early Access; broader-scope comparison)

Source:
- https://steamcommunity.com/games/1621690/announcements/detail/3345626689196513649

Verified success:
- 100,000 copies in first two days of Early Access
- 1,000,000 copies by June 30, 2022

Core:
- mining is embedded in a much larger survival/crafting/co-op sandbox

Why include it:
- it confirms extremely broad demand for discovery-through-excavation
- biomes and things found underground can sustain long-term curiosity

Why **not** imitate it:
- its content scale is the opposite of this project
- crafting, farming, combat, multiplayer and base-building would destroy our small-scope advantage

Take only:
- meaningful biome/depth discovery
- visible promise of "something new beyond this wall"

# Cross-case pattern

Successful digging games repeatedly reduce to:

**simple physical verb**
+
**clear reason to return**
+
**big upgrade**
+
**visible promise below**
+
**one identity twist**

Examples of identity twist:
- AGADAH: pure 3D hole / radical simplicity
- SteamWorld Dig: platforming
- Dome Keeper: timed defense
- Wall World: mobile wall-climbing base
- Mining Mechs: co-op mechs
- DigDigDrill: custom drill buildcraft
- Bore Blasters: machine-gun excavation

## flesh-pit's identity twist

We already have several connected pieces that behave like one twist:

> **You literally consume the terrain. Your stomach is the inventory limit. The toilet is the return point. Repetition mutates your body.**

Do not bury this under additional genre systems.

# Production implications

## 1. Trailer test

The game should be understandable in ~10 seconds:
- tear/eat flesh
- stomach meter fills
- rush back
- vomit
- mutation appears
- return and tear flesh differently

If a trailer needs lore explanation first, the design has drifted.

## 2. Upgrade test

Every major upgrade should pass:
**can a viewer see the difference without reading text?**

## 3. Depth test

Every major depth band should add at least one new excavation decision.

## 4. Return test

Returning must create anticipation, not feel like punishment.

## 5. Scope test

If a proposed feature cannot strengthen:
- eating feel
- stomach greed
- return/vomit rhythm
- mutation
- depth discovery

cut or postpone it.

# One-line synthesis

> **The successful digging games do not win by making digging complicated; they make a stupidly legible digging verb collide with one strong constraint or progression fantasy.**
